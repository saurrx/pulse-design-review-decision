Semantic usage rules
SPEC 3.1 — NORMATIVE
3.1.1
GREEN
Terminal success only. Reserve pl-green-* for states with no further action — Granted. Never use green for in-progress milestones.
3.1.2
BLUE
Informational, in-flight. pl-blue-* marks neutral procedural states — Filed, Sent to IP Committee — where the matter is moving but no attorney action is required.
3.1.3
AMBER
Attention, action pending. pl-amber-* signals a required response — Review Pending, Update Requested. Escalate to red only when a deadline passes.
3.1.4
RED
Blocking or failed. pl-red-* is limited to Overdue and Rejected by QC. A red chip must always be paired with a recovery action in the UI.
3.1.5
SLATE
No judgment yet — drafts and archived items must recede. pl-slate-* is reserved for Draft and archived matters that carry no workflow signal.
