# Motion

Motion in Pulse is noticeable but professional. It explains change, progress,
and relationship; it is never spectacle.

## Use motion for

- Source material becoming structured disclosure content
- Prefilled fields appearing in place
- Completion progress
- Autosave acknowledgement
- Evaluation starting, progressing, and completing
- Opening evidence through progressive disclosure
- Submitting for review
- Approving and sending to Photon Legal
- Action status changing
- Context-preserving navigation between list and detail

## Avoid motion for

- Background decoration
- Continuous attention-seeking dashboard effects
- Legal-risk emphasis through bouncing, pulsing, or shaking
- Confetti
- Child-like celebration
- Large parallax or cinematic transitions inside daily work
- Animation that delays reading or clicking

## Behavior

- Motion follows the originating control and makes cause/effect clear.
- Prefer opacity and transform.
- Never use `transition: all`.
- Animations are interruptible.
- Loading indicators do not flash for trivial waits.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with an equivalent non-motion state.
- No sound in V0.
