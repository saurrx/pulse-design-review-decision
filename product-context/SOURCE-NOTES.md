# Source Notes and Reconciliation

This package distinguishes founder-confirmed V0 intent from historical source
material. Historical documents are evidence, not automatic authority.

## Founder-confirmed authority

- V0 is the first production release of a clean rebuild.
- Pulse is free with Photon Legal patent services and is not SaaS.
- The ICP has legal leadership but no dedicated internal IP counsel team.
- The primary commercial mechanism is more ideas reaching paid patent work.
- V0 has four personas.
- V0 is authorized as a complete UI/UX redesign.
- PL-TKN-004 is the binding visual starting point, with Photon colors retained.
- Trademark and cost visibility are later roadmap.

## PRD reconciliation

### PRD 1 — Redesigned authentication

Adopted for V0: domain-gated access, four auth methods, invitations, QR/bulk
invites, recovery, role/access audit expectations. Old role names are replaced
by the four V0 personas.

### PRD 2 — Onboarding

Adopted for V0, including the complete Workspace Admin and Inventor activation
email sequences. Self-signup is also allowed for an onboarded domain.

### PRD 3 — Submission

Adopted with founder clarification: upload/paste and prefill are core; copilot
improvements and follow-up questions are included but secondary; evaluation is
optional and user-triggered; re-evaluation is allowed; every score may submit.

### PRD 4 — Admin workspace

Adopted as Workspace Admin review guidance. Pending reviews lead, review is one
stage, and approval means send to Photon Legal for filing.

### PRD 5 — Trademark

Not V0. Retained only as later-roadmap background.

## Legacy-product reconciliation

The legacy product was audited in Photon Admin and client modes. Its major
objects—Ideas, Patents, Actions, Clients, Workspace—remain useful. Its layout,
dashboards, broad metrics, world map, dense tables, and detailed information
hierarchy are not design authority.

The temporary compromise in the founder call to preserve the old UI was later
explicitly superseded. V0 should be substantially better across all included
screens while retaining recognizable objects where helpful.

## Founder-feedback reconciliation

The raw call contained competing real-time ideas. The authoritative decisions
are the later founder validations recorded in this package:

- Numeric score remains 0–10.
- No cutoff applies.
- Evaluation is advisory and optional.
- Inventor evidence is progressively disclosed.
- World maps were removed from V0 dashboards in 1.0.0 and restored on every
  dashboard in 1.1.0 (see VALIDATION.md).
- Cost visibility is later.
- Full V0 visual/UX redesign is authorized.
