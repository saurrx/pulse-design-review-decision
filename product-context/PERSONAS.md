# Personas and Jobs

V0 has exactly four product personas. Do not import the six-role model from a
previous implementation.

## 1. Inventor

### Who they are

An employee, engineer, researcher, product builder, or technical leader who may
have created something worth protecting. Patent work is not their daily job.

### Job to be done

Turn an idea and scattered supporting material into a disclosure that the
company can review and Photon Legal can understand.

### Context and anxiety

- They may not know patent vocabulary.
- They may not know what detail is sufficient.
- A traditional questionnaire feels like work unrelated to their main job.
- They fear saying something incorrectly or wasting time.
- They may start with a deck, technical document, notes, code, or pasted text.

### Desired feeling

Inspired, guided, capable, reassured, and proud of contributing.

### Success

- Starts with almost no friction
- Sees useful prefilled content quickly
- Understands gaps without feeling judged
- Completes and submits confidently
- Understands the advisory evaluation
- Knows what happens after submission

### Permissions

- Domain self-signup or invitation
- Create and manage own/credited ideas
- Add co-inventors
- Upload or paste source material
- Evaluate and re-evaluate
- Submit at any score
- Respond to requested changes and resubmit
- View own idea timeline
- View the complete company patent portfolio read-only
- Cannot see due dates, Actions, clients, or administration

## 2. Workspace Admin

### Who they are

Usually the General Counsel or legal leader, and sometimes the CTO, innovation
leader, or members of an internal review/IP committee. A workspace may have
several Workspace Admins.

### Job to be done

Encourage invention submissions, review them efficiently, decide which ideas
should be filed, and maintain visibility over the company's program and patent
portfolio.

### Context and anxiety

- Patent review is one responsibility among many.
- A growing queue can become invisible in email or spreadsheets.
- Decisions should be defensible without requiring a full internal IP team.
- They need enough evidence to decide, not every available data point.

### Desired feeling

Focused, confident, decisive, and informed.

### Success

- Pending reviews are unmistakable
- Oldest work and days waiting are visible
- A decision can be made from one coherent screen
- The consequence of approval is explicit
- Portfolio and upcoming responsibilities are easy to understand

### Permissions

- See all ideas in the workspace
- Submit own ideas
- Submit on behalf of a selected real inventor
- Review, request changes, reject, or approve for filing
- Invite and manage workspace users
- View the patent portfolio and patent details read-only
- View and submit patent-event Actions
- See due dates within Actions and patent details
- Cannot import or directly edit patent/due-date records

## 3. Case Owner

### Who they are

A Photon Legal team member responsible for a set of assigned client companies.

### Job to be done

Keep assigned clients onboarded, supported, and moving from approved ideas to
organized Photon Legal work while maintaining accurate portfolio visibility.

### Desired feeling

Prepared, responsive, and in control.

### Success

- Sees which assigned client needs attention
- Receives approved ideas as clear briefs
- Can find attachments and review history immediately
- Can maintain portfolio and dates without crossing client boundaries
- Can process assigned Actions without hunting

### Permissions

- Assigned clients only
- Help onboard clients
- Add Workspace Admins and invite inventors
- View assigned-client ideas, portfolio, Actions, and dates
- Receive approved ideas
- Import and manually update assigned-client patents and due dates
- Process assigned-client Action requests
- Enter client view when authorized
- Does not normally make the client's internal review decision

## 4. Photon Admin

### Who they are

A Photon Legal administrator with firm-wide operational responsibility.

### Job to be done

Configure clients and access, allocate Case Owners, maintain data quality, and
ensure firm-wide client work is visible and controlled.

### Desired feeling

Calm, informed, precise, and confident that nothing is being missed.

### Success

- Client ownership and access are clear
- Unassigned or stuck work is visible
- Firm-level queues prioritize action over vanity metrics
- Data corrections are possible and auditable

### Permissions

- See all clients and their V0 data
- Create and configure clients
- Assign Case Owners
- Manage Photon users and access
- View all approved ideas, portfolios, dates, and Actions
- Import and manually update all client patents and due dates
- Process Action requests
- Does not normally replace the client's Workspace Admin review
