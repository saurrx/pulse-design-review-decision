# Business Context

## Commercial model

Clients do not pay for Pulse. Legal leadership pays Photon Legal for patent
services on a per-matter basis. Pulse is included with those services.

Photon Legal's commercial proposition includes:

1. Cost clarity: a patent matter has a fixed service price irrespective of the
   number of office actions, subject to the governing commercial terms.
2. Greater outcome certainty: the service includes a money-back commitment if
   patent protection cannot be obtained, subject to the governing terms.

The product must not expand or reinterpret those legal/commercial promises.

## Revenue mechanism

Pulse generates revenue indirectly:

```text
More inventors invited and activated
→ More invention submissions started
→ More drafts completed
→ More ideas submitted
→ Faster client review
→ More ideas approved for filing
→ More paid patent matters for Photon Legal
```

## Primary V0 priorities

1. More inventors begin a submission.
2. More started drafts are completed.
3. More completed drafts are submitted.
4. More inventors participate in the program.
5. Workspace Admins review faster.
6. More approved ideas reach Photon Legal.

Portfolio visibility, lifecycle visibility, Actions, and onboarding support the
service relationship, but are not the primary V0 growth loop.

## Product-value story

For inventors, Pulse removes blank-page anxiety and converts material they
already have into a manageable disclosure.

For Workspace Admins, Pulse makes the review queue clear and decisions fast.

For Photon Legal, Pulse creates a consistent, information-rich intake for more
potential patent matters.

For legal leadership, Pulse shows the program, ideas, portfolio, and lifecycle
without requiring an internal IP operations team.

## Business test for a design

Every material design must answer:

- Which user behavior should change?
- Why should that behavior increase submission, review, conversion, retention,
  trust, or operational efficiency?
- What observable signal will show whether the change worked?

A design that receives compliments but changes no valuable user behavior has
not yet proved business value.
