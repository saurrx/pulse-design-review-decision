# Product and Design Metrics

## North-star relationship

Pulse should increase the number of qualified ideas that reach Photon Legal for
filing. Revenue is realized outside Pulse through paid patent matters.

## Primary funnel

1. Inventor activated
2. Submission started
3. Draft completed
4. Idea submitted
5. Review completed
6. Idea sent to Photon Legal
7. Converted to paid patent matter
8. Filed

## Definitions

- **Inventor activated:** first successful entry into their company workspace.
- **Submission started:** an idea record is created after title, paste, or upload.
- **Draft completed:** all required invention-disclosure information is complete.
- **Idea submitted:** disclosure is sent to Workspace Admin review.
- **Review completed:** Approve, Request changes, or Reject is recorded.
- **Sent to Photon:** Workspace Admin approves the disclosure for filing.
- **Converted to paid patent matter:** Photon accepts it into the external
  drafting/filing service workflow.
- **Filed:** application/filing information is added to Pulse.

## Primary V0 measures

- Submission-start rate per activated inventor
- Draft completion rate
- Completed-to-submitted conversion
- Median time from start to completion
- Median time from completion to submission
- Inventors submitting at least one idea per workspace
- Workspace Admin pending-review age
- Median submission-to-decision time
- Approved-to-Photon conversion

## Experience measures

- Task completion without assistance
- Time on task
- Wrong turns and abandoned steps
- Recovery from errors
- Confidence after submission and review
- Perceived clarity of novelty score
- Ability to identify what makes the idea different
- Support contacts per completed submission

## Guardrails

- A higher submission count must not come from low-quality empty material.
- Evaluation must not reduce submission by appearing mandatory.
- Social proof must not discourage lower-ranked employees.
- Faster review must not hide evidence or weaken decision quality.
- Email activation must not create unacceptable unsubscribe or complaint rates.
- Do not optimize time-in-product; efficient work may reduce it.

## Goals, signals, metrics

Each design brief contains:

```text
Goal: the user/business outcome
Signal: observable behavior indicating progress
Metric: the calculation used to measure it
Guardrail: what must not worsen
```
