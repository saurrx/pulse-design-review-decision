# Anti-patterns

## Generic AI output

- Purple/blue gradient hero language inside product screens
- Bento cards for every piece of information
- Oversized headings that reduce working space
- Decorative glows, blobs, glass, and arbitrary color
- Generic copy such as `Unlock insights` or `Supercharge innovation`
- Fake charts and metrics without a user decision
- Chat interface used as a substitute for product interaction
- Excessive explanation the user must read before acting

## Legacy enterprise software

- Spreadsheet density without task hierarchy
- Raw identifiers and system statuses
- Tables containing every available column
- Tiny controls and weak contrast
- Modal chains
- Unclear ownership and next steps
- Dashboards dominated by totals and maps
- Separate screens for information that belongs in context

## Hollow delight

- Confetti after routine legal work
- Pulsing or bouncing urgency
- Motion that delays action
- Leaderboards that shame or discourage inventors
- Friendly copy that hides consequence
- Color used instead of clear labels

## False AI authority

- Patentability guarantees
- Probability labels unsupported by the evaluation
- A single score with no explanation
- All ideas or prior-art items receiving the same-looking score
- Detailed evidence presented before the conclusion
- Repeated disclaimers that make the product sound uncertain

## Business misalignment

- Optimizing time in product rather than task completion
- Making evaluation look mandatory
- Prioritizing portfolio spectacle over starting an idea
- Prioritizing total patent counts over pending review
- Adding roadmap modules to make the product appear larger
- Treating visual compliments as proof of revenue impact
