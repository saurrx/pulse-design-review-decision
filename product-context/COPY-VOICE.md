# Copy and Voice

## Voice by context

### Inventor

Supportive, plain-spoken, encouraging, and respectful of technical expertise.
Explain patent-process concepts without sounding remedial. Reduce anxiety and
make the next step manageable.

### Workspace Admin

Concise, decision-oriented, and precise. Make ownership, age, evidence, and
consequence easy to scan.

### Case Owner

Operational and responsive. Use client, object, owner, status, and next action
consistently.

### Photon Admin

Compact and exact. Exceptions, access, ownership, and data-quality consequences
must be unambiguous.

### AI evaluation

Confident, evidence-led, direct, and useful. State once that the assessment is
AI-assisted and advisory. Do not repeat cautious boilerplate around every
conclusion. Do not imply certainty, objectivity, or a guarantee.

## Writing rules

- Use active voice and familiar words.
- Introduce as few product terms as possible.
- Use one noun for one object across every surface.
- Use sentence case unless the design system explicitly defines otherwise.
- Prefer specific actions: `Approve for filing`, not `Continue`.
- Explain errors beside the affected field and state the recovery action.
- Keep headings informative rather than motivational filler.
- Never expose internal enums, IDs, endpoint language, or implementation terms.
- Use `prior art`, never `prior arts`.
- Do not describe a novelty score as a probability of receiving a patent.

## Evaluation example

Good:

> 6.8 / 10 — Your disclosure appears to contain meaningful differences from
> the closest prior art. Strengthen the authority-transfer mechanism with one
> concrete implementation example.

Avoid:

> Highly likely to be patented.

Avoid:

> Based on our objective AI analysis, this invention is patentable.

## Email rules

- Subject lines state the object or task.
- Emails contain one primary action.
- Reminders explain why returning is worthwhile.
- Do not manufacture urgency.
- Digests summarize counts and oldest age, then link to the queue.
- Provide legally and operationally required preferences/unsubscribe controls.
