# Product Context

## What Pulse is

Pulse is a free product delivered with Photon Legal services. It gives a client
company one place to capture invention ideas, evaluate them, review them,
send approved ideas to Photon Legal for filing, and understand the resulting
patent portfolio and lifecycle.

Pulse replaces Excel sheets, email threads, scattered documents, and manual
follow-up. It is not positioned as a replacement for enterprise IP management
systems.

## Ideal customer profile

The primary client is a company that:

- Has a General Counsel or legal leader
- Does not have a dedicated internal IP counsel team
- Outsources patent drafting, filing, and related legal work to Photon Legal
- Currently coordinates invention intake and portfolio information manually
- Wants more employees to disclose valuable inventions
- Needs simple visibility without operating a full docketing function

## V0 definition

V0 is the first production release of a clean rebuild. The old product is not
the codebase or visual structure to preserve. Familiar product objects and
terminology may remain when they help users, but every V0 screen may be
reconsidered and redesigned.

## Core V0 capabilities

- Domain-gated self-signup
- Invitation, QR, bulk email, and inventor CSV onboarding
- Invention capture from title, pasted text, or uploaded source material
- Structured invention disclosure with prefilled answers
- Copilot follow-up questions and answer-improvement suggestions
- Optional prior-art evaluation and re-evaluation
- Numeric novelty score with evidence
- Workspace Admin review
- Request changes, reject, approve for filing, and resubmission
- Company patent portfolio and patent details
- Patent lifecycle visibility
- Patent-event Actions and contextual due dates
- Manual and spreadsheet patent/due-date maintenance by Photon roles
- Client onboarding and role/access management
- Activation, reminder, review, and digest emails
- Selected operational and portfolio summaries

## Explicitly outside V0

- Selling Pulse as SaaS
- Checkout, purchasing, billing, invoices, or price selection
- Performing patent drafting or filing inside Pulse
- Initial filing-instruction purchase flow
- Full docketing or a docketing-system integration
- Client editing/importing patent and due-date data
- Inventor access to Actions or due dates
- Trademark management
- Cost visibility and revenue analytics
- General-purpose AI assistant
- Document-management system
- Notification center or bell
- Mobile and tablet layouts

## Later roadmap

- Trademark module
- Cost and invoice visibility
- Additional legal-service modules
- Deeper portfolio analytics
- Integrations with external legal and patent-office systems

Roadmap material must never silently appear in a V0 design.
