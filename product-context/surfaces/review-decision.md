# Review Decision

## Persona

Workspace Admin.

## Job

Understand an invention quickly enough to make a defensible client decision.

## Business outcome

Faster review and more suitable ideas sent to Photon Legal.

## Primary hierarchy

1. Invention brief and current state
2. Inventor and attribution
3. Novelty assessment and meaning
4. What appears different
5. Key disclosure answers and attachments
6. Detailed prior art on demand
7. Activity/history

## Decisions

- Primary: **Approve for filing**
- Secondary: **Request changes**
- Destructive/negative: **Reject**

Approval confirmation:

- Title: **Send to Photon Legal for filing?**
- Primary action: **Approve & send**
- Result: **Sent to Photon Legal**

## Rules

- One decision workspace; no extra preview screen.
- Admin cannot silently edit submitted inventor content.
- Request and rejection require useful reasons.
- If another Admin has already decided, show the recorded outcome and actor.
- Preserve context after opening attachments or evidence.

## States

Typical, no evaluation, partial evaluation, long disclosure, missing detail,
decision in progress, concurrent decision completed, success, failure/retry.
