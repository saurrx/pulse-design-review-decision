# Inventor Home

## Job

Start an idea or understand what happened to existing ideas.

## Business outcome

Increase submission starts and inventor participation.

## Primary action

**Submit an idea** — persistent and unmistakable.

## Hierarchy

1. My ideas with the next step for each (continue a draft, send an evaluated
   idea, see what review decided), with **Submit an idea** as the one primary
   button beside them
2. My pipeline: ideas by stage
3. Collective innovation momentum (submissions this quarter, ideas reaching
   filing) without named ranking
4. Company patent portfolio: compact status summary and the patent world map,
   below the fold of the ideas list

## Motivation

Show collective social proof such as ideas submitted this quarter and ideas
that progressed to filing. Never rank named inventors here.

## Exclude

Due dates, Actions, firm administration, generic patent totals that do not
help the inventor act, a Top inventors list, and a dashboard grid of
equal-weight cards. The map is included but never above the ideas.

## States

First run, invited but inactive, no ideas, one active draft, several idea
statuses, requested changes, recent submission, evaluation available, error.
