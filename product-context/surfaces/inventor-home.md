# Inventor Home

## Job

Start an idea or understand what happened to existing ideas.

## Business outcome

Increase submission starts and inventor participation.

## Primary action

**Submit an idea** — persistent and unmistakable.

## Hierarchy

1. Submit an idea
2. Continue an unfinished disclosure, if one exists
3. Status and next step for the inventor's ideas
4. Collective innovation momentum
5. Compact company portfolio entry point

## Motivation

Show collective social proof such as ideas submitted this quarter and ideas
that progressed to filing. Do not rank named inventors by default.

## Exclude

World map, due dates, Actions, firm administration, generic patent totals that
do not help the inventor act, and a dashboard grid of equal-weight cards.

## States

First run, invited but inactive, no ideas, one active draft, several idea
statuses, requested changes, recent submission, evaluation available, error.
