# Idea Detail and Status

## Job

Understand the invention disclosure, its evidence, history, ownership, and
current next step.

## Persona emphasis

- Inventor: feedback, status, next step, and what happens after submission.
- Workspace Admin: decision support.
- Case Owner/Photon Admin: usable filing brief and attachments.

## Information

- Human-readable idea reference and title
- Primary inventor, co-inventors, and submitted-by attribution
- Concise brief
- Full disclosure by section
- Source material and attachments
- Evaluation summary and detailed evidence
- Review decisions/comments
- Activity and status timeline
- Related patent after filing

## Avoid

Rendering the entire questionnaire as one undifferentiated wall, repeating the
same score in several places, internal IDs, or actions unavailable to the role.

## States

Draft, evaluated, submitted, under review, changes requested, rejected,
resubmitted, sent to Photon, filed, granted, closed, missing evaluation,
partial evaluation.
