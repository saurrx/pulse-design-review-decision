# Start an Idea

## Job

Move from “I may have an invention” to a useful starting disclosure with
almost no blank-page friction.

## Business outcome

Increase submission-start rate.

## Primary action

Start with material the inventor already has.

## Inputs

- Title
- Paste text
- Upload decks, documents, notes, or other supported material
- Start manually if desired

## Hierarchy

1. Plain invitation to describe or bring the idea
2. Large paste/upload affordance
3. Minimal title requirement
4. Supported formats and privacy reassurance
5. What Pulse will do next

## Defining moment

After input, Pulse visibly organizes the material into a structured disclosure.
Use noticeable but professional motion to maintain continuity from source to
prefilled answers.

## Avoid

Long questionnaire first, patent jargon, mandatory evaluation language,
decorative upload animation, or suggesting that AI will invent missing facts.

## States

Empty, dragging file, parsing, partial extraction, unsupported file, extraction
failure, duplicate idea warning, successful transition to disclosure.
