# Authentication and Access

## Personas

All users.

## Job

Enter the correct workspace securely with minimal uncertainty.

## Business outcome

More invited and domain-eligible inventors activate successfully.

## Screens

- Login
- Domain-gated signup
- Invitation acceptance
- Forgot/reset password
- SSO return/failure
- Access denied/session expired

## Hierarchy

1. Photon Legal/Pulse identity and clear purpose
2. Recommended authentication choices
3. Email/password fallback where supported
4. Recovery and signup
5. Trust/reassurance without legal boilerplate

## Rules

- Self-signup accepts only onboarded domains.
- Invitation is not the only entry path.
- Google, Microsoft, SSO, and email remain available as product requirements.
- Do not show an empty half-page where a removed brand panel used to be.
- Avoid four actions competing with equal visual weight.
- Preserve entered values after recoverable errors.
- Explain domain rejection and session expiry with a next step.

## States

Default, loading, invalid credentials, unknown domain, expired invitation,
successful invitation, SSO unavailable, SSO failure, reset requested, reset
complete, session expired.
