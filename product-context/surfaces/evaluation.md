# Evaluation Result

## Job

Understand the advisory patentability assessment and strengthen the disclosure.

## Business outcome

Help inventors submit stronger disclosures without reducing submission volume.

## Primary hierarchy

### 1. Assessment

Numeric 0–10 patentability score (`7.4 / 10`), its band label (Highly,
Moderately or Marginally novel; Closely matched) and one plain-language
interpretation. No cutoff and no implication that the score predicts patent
grant. Inside the draft, before a full evaluation exists, a patentability
signal may read the written sections back; it is labelled as a signal, not a
score.

### 2. What appears different

Two or three meaningful differentiators from the closest prior art.

### 3. How to strengthen

Concrete prompts tied to missing or ambiguous technical detail.

### Detailed prior art

Collapsed by default. Include ranked references, similarity, per-reference
analysis, overlaps, differences, evidence, coverage, and provenance.

## Actions

- Return to disclosure
- Re-evaluate after meaningful edits
- Submit for review at any score
- Open/download detailed report where supported

## Voice

Confident and evidence-led. State once that this is an AI-assisted advisory
assessment. Avoid timid repetition and false legal certainty.

## States

Not run, queued, running, succeeded, partial/degraded, no close prior art,
failed, timed out, stale after edits, re-evaluating.
