# Case Owner — My Work

## Job

Know which assigned client needs attention and move approved work forward.

## Business outcome

Reduce coordination delay between client approval and Photon service delivery.

## Hierarchy

1. Approved ideas newly sent to Photon
2. Urgent assigned-client Actions and due dates
3. Client onboarding or access tasks
4. Assigned clients and current health
5. Recent lifecycle updates

## Rules

- Assigned-client scope is always clear.
- Work leads; aggregate metrics recede.
- Every item identifies client, object, age/urgency, and next step.
- Client view is accessible but visibly distinct and easy to exit.
- The patent world map appears below the work, assigned-client scope, never
  leading. Do not show a global client ranking.
- No navigation badge; awaiting-action counts live on My Work.

## States

No assigned clients, newly assigned client, new approved idea, urgent action,
overdue date, onboarding incomplete, access expired, data error.
