# Photon Due Dates

## Personas

Case Owner and Photon Admin. Workspace Admin sees dates contextually in Actions
and patent details. Inventor never sees them.

## Job

Find upcoming or overdue client patent events and maintain accurate visibility.

## Data source

Manual Photon update and spreadsheet import only. No docketing integration.

## Hierarchy

- Urgency/days remaining
- Event
- Patent/application
- Client
- Owner
- Date
- Status
- Related Action

## Views

Default list optimized for urgency; calendar may be secondary when it helps
capacity planning. Large calendars must not hide the actionable queue.

## States

Upcoming, due soon, overdue, completed, missing date, disputed/import problem,
large same-day group, no upcoming dates, loading, error.
