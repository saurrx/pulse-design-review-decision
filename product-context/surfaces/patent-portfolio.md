# Patent Portfolio

## Personas

All four personas, with different permissions and scope.

## Job

Understand the company's patent assets and find a specific record quickly.

## Scope

- Inventor and Workspace Admin: own company, read-only
- Case Owner: assigned clients, import/edit
- Photon Admin: all clients, import/edit

## Information hierarchy

- Search and high-value filters
- Human-readable application/reference numbers
- Title
- Status
- Jurisdiction
- Filing/application date
- Inventor/assignee where useful
- Upcoming date indicator only for authorized non-Inventor personas

## Portfolio summary

Use compact totals and jurisdiction distribution. Prefer counts, filters, and
clear status groupings over a world map.

## Rules

- Do not recreate a spreadsheet by showing every field at once.
- Column configuration may exist for operational Photon roles.
- Preserve export where currently required.
- Import belongs only to Case Owner and Photon Admin.

## States

Empty imported portfolio, typical, thousands of records, long titles, multiple
jurisdictions, filtered empty, import result, loading, error.
