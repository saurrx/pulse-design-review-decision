# Patent Detail

## Job

Understand one patent's identity, legal/lifecycle state, family, documents, and
relationship to the originating idea.

## Information

- Patent/application title and human-readable identifiers
- Status and jurisdiction
- Key dates
- Inventors and assignee
- Abstract
- Family members
- Documents
- Lifecycle timeline
- Related invention disclosure when available
- Upcoming dates and Actions for authorized roles
- Notes for roles allowed to maintain them

## Persona differences

- Inventor: read-only and no due dates.
- Workspace Admin: read-only with contextual dates and Actions.
- Case Owner/Photon Admin: edit/import-related controls and data-quality context.

## Avoid

Equal-weight sections, raw database fields, duplicated status, or due-date
information shown to Inventors.

## States

Pending, filed, granted, inactive, closed, incomplete imported record, multiple
family members, no originating idea, many documents, upcoming event.
