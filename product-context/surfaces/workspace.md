# Workspace, People, and Profile

## Workspace Admin job

Configure the organization and get inventors participating.

## Workspace areas

- Organization identity and logo
- Allowed domain
- Business scope
- Workspace Admins
- Inventors
- Invitations
- Short link and QR
- Bulk email paste
- Inventor CSV import
- User status and role management

## Business outcome

More eligible inventors enter the workspace and begin submissions.

## Hierarchy

1. Participation and next onboarding action
2. People and invitation status
3. Organization information
4. Business scope

## Profile

Personal information, authentication methods, password where applicable, and
email preferences. Keep profile distinct from organization administration.

## States

New workspace, no inventors, invitations pending, active team, expired link,
CSV errors, suspended user, domain conflict, save error.
