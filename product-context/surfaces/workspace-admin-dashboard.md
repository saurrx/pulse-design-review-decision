# Workspace Admin Dashboard

## Job

Clear the review queue and understand whether the invention program is moving.

## Business outcome

Reduce submission-to-decision time and increase ideas sent to Photon.

## Hierarchy

1. Needs your review: title, inventor, score, days waiting
2. Review all / open next review
3. Idea pipeline: submitted, review pending, sent to Photon, filed, granted
4. Innovation momentum: submissions and filings trend
5. Compact patent-portfolio summary

## Rules

- Pending reviews occupy prime space before aggregate metrics.
- Pending sorts oldest first.
- Clearly distinguish workspace pipeline from company patent portfolio.
- No world map.
- No cost analytics in V0.
- Ideas navigation carries pending-review count.

## States

No pending reviews, one urgent review, large aging queue, no recent ideas,
healthy growth, declining submissions, data unavailable.
