# Pulse Design Philosophy

## Emotional signature

Pulse should feel calm enough for legal work, encouraging enough for inventors,
decisive enough for reviewers, operational enough for Photon staff, and clear
enough that advisory AI builds trust rather than mystery.

The V0 personality is:

- Calm
- Credible
- Premium
- Intelligent
- Human
- Precise
- Encouraging
- Professionally expressive

It must not feel old, aqua/glassy, visually loud, child-like, excessively
animated, like spreadsheet decoration, like a generic SaaS template, or like
an AI chatbot wrapper.

## Principles

### 1. One task leads

Every screen has one unmistakable reason to exist. The primary task receives
the strongest position and contrast. Navigation and supporting information
recede once orientation is established.

### 2. Start with material, not a blank form

Inventors should begin with a title, pasted content, or files they already
have. The product creates momentum by organizing that material into a useful
disclosure rather than confronting the inventor with a long empty questionnaire.

### 3. Transformation is the defining delight

The signature moment is seeing scattered material become a structured,
credible starting disclosure. Delight comes from feeling capable and making
progress, not from decorative celebration.

### 4. Evidence earns attention

Show conclusions first, then plain-language meaning, then the most actionable
evidence. Detailed provenance remains easy to inspect but does not overwhelm
the first view.

### 5. Progress is always visible

Users know what was saved, what is complete, what is missing, what is running,
who owns the next step, and what happens after an action.

### 6. High-stakes work is forgiving

Preserve entered work. Make important consequences explicit. Confirm destructive
or externally consequential actions. Keep activity history. Help users recover.

### 7. Familiar objects, reconsidered composition

Ideas, patents, Actions, clients, and workspaces remain understandable product
objects. Their visual composition and navigation may be redesigned when doing
so improves the job. Legacy placement is not authority.

### 8. Quiet at rest, expressive in response

Most of the product remains stable and calm. Motion and emphasis appear when
the system acknowledges input, reveals transformation, communicates progress,
or confirms a meaningful completion.

### 9. Social proof without unhealthy ranking

Encourage invention through collective company momentum: submissions, progress,
and ideas reaching filing. Avoid ranking named employees by default; leaderboards
can discourage participation or create the wrong incentives.

### 10. The product serves the business through the user

The interface should increase valuable behavior, not merely look impressive.
Every design identifies the user behavior and business outcome it intends to
improve.

## Brand direction

Pulse is a Photon Legal product and uses the same core brand identity. The
Photon navy and amber/yellow remain recognizable. PL-TKN-004 is the binding V0
starting point. Typography and semantic token organization may improve, but
core brand color changes require explicit approval.

The visual system favors strong typography, disciplined hierarchy, hairline
separation, restrained surfaces, and precise spacing. Avoid gratuitous shadows,
glows, translucency, gradients, and containers around every block.
