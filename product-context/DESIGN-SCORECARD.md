# Pulse Design Scorecard

Score each category 1–5 after inspecting the rendered interface. Explain every
score below 4 and address the largest gaps before presenting the design.

The score is a critique aid, not proof of user or business success.

## Product fit

- Is the persona and job explicit?
- Is the screen's reason for existing obvious?
- Does the interface preserve V0 permissions and workflow?
- Is the intended business behavior named?
- Has roadmap scope stayed out of V0?

## Hierarchy

- Is one primary task unmistakable?
- Does secondary UI recede?
- Is metric scope clear: personal, workspace, client, or firm?
- Is prime space used for something actionable or motivating?
- Can the screen be understood quickly?

## Usability

- Are next steps and consequences explicit?
- Can the user recover without losing work?
- Are loading, empty, error, dense, and long-content states coherent?
- Does navigation preserve context?
- Are complex details progressively disclosed?

## Trust

- Are evaluation conclusions explained by evidence?
- Is advisory AI labelled once without timid repetition?
- Are important actions attributable and auditable?
- Does the product avoid guarantees or false objectivity?
- Does the design feel appropriate for confidential IP work?

## Craft

- Are typography, spacing, borders, icons, and alignment deliberate?
- Are controls and states consistent?
- Does anything look like a generic AI-generated SaaS template?
- Has every card, label, metric, and animation earned its place?
- Is the Photon Legal identity recognizable without overwhelming the task?

## Accessibility and resilience

- Keyboard, focus, semantics, contrast, and zoom?
- Reduced-motion equivalent?
- Long titles, large totals, missing data, and localization?
- No information conveyed by color alone?
- No internal IDs or raw system language?

## Business

- Which behavior should improve?
- What metric detects it?
- What guardrail prevents a hollow improvement?
- Does the design reduce friction in the submission-to-filing revenue loop?
- Is there a simpler intervention with equal impact?
